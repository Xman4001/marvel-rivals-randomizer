A little chaos. Your rules.

  1. Build your composition. Choose Vanguard, Duelist, and Strategist counts. Each team can have 1–6 players.
  2. Put names to the picks. Enter player names beside each slot. Names stay in place when you reroll.
  3. Roll, lock, repeat. Randomize both teams, lock a pick you like, or reroll a single unlocked hero with its refresh button.
  4. Take it to your group. Copy both lineups and paste them into your chat.
One hero, one pick. Heroes never repeat across the two teams. Deadpool can fill any of his three roles, but appears only once per match.

Names, settings, and your latest lineup are saved in this browser when local storage is available. Your GitHub roster refreshes automatically; a bundled roster keeps the app usable if GitHub is unavailable.
